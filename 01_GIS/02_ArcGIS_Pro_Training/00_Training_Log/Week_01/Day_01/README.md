# Day 01 — GIS Course & ArcGIS Pro Introduction

**Date:** 06.07.2026  
**Week:** 01 — GIS & ArcGIS Pro Foundations

## Overview

Day 01 marked the beginning of the GIS training program and established the organizational and technical foundations for the following weeks.

The training is structured around GIS, 3D analysis, and Spatial Analyst workflows using ArcGIS Pro.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Course Introduction

- Introduction to the training program
- Participant introductions
- Professional backgrounds and GIS experience
- Individual professional goals
- Overview of the 8-week and 12-week training tracks

### 2. Training Environment

Introduction to the technical learning environment:

- Alfaview
- Remote PCs
- Digital learning environment
- ArcGIS Pro installation
- Course data exchange environment

### 3. ArcGIS Pro Access

ArcGIS Pro access was established through the provided training environment.

The software was available on the remote training PC and accessed through the ArcGIS Pro desktop application.

### 4. Learning Resources

The following resources were introduced:

- ArcGIS Pro German-language handbook
- ArcGIS Pro Quick Start resources
- ArcGIS Lesson Gallery
- Training presentations and course materials
