
# Day 04 — DLM & Digital Terrain Models

**Date:** 09.07.2026  
**Week:** 01 — GIS & ArcGIS Pro Foundations

## Overview

Day 04 focused on Digital Landscape Models (DLM) and Digital Terrain Models (DTM) as important foundations for working with geospatial and three-dimensional data in ArcGIS Pro.

The day introduced the role of terrain-related datasets in GIS workflows and their use for representing geographic surfaces and landscape information.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Digital Landscape Models (DLM)

- Introduction to Digital Landscape Models
- Representation of geographic and landscape information
- Structure and organization of landscape-related geospatial data
- Use of DLM data within GIS workflows

### 2. Digital Terrain Models (DTM)

- Introduction to Digital Terrain Models
- Representation of terrain surfaces
- Understanding elevation-related geospatial information
- Working with terrain data in ArcGIS Pro

### 3. Terrain & 3D Concepts

The training introduced the relationship between geospatial data and three-dimensional terrain representation.

Key concepts included:

- Elevation
- Terrain surfaces
- Digital terrain representation
- 3D visualization
- Spatial reference of terrain data

## Practical Learning

The focus was on understanding how terrain and landscape information can be represented and used within ArcGIS Pro.

DLM and DTM concepts provide an important foundation for subsequent GIS workflows involving elevation, terrain analysis, three-dimensional visualization, and spatial analysis.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Understand the purpose of Digital Landscape Models
- Understand the purpose of Digital Terrain Models
- Distinguish between landscape information and terrain representation
- Understand the role of elevation data in GIS
- Understand the relationship between terrain data and 3D visualization
- Recognize the importance of terrain models for spatial analysis
