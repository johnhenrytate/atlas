
# Day 05 — Geodata Formats, Infrastructure & Services

**Date:** 10.07.2026  
**Week:** 01 — GIS & ArcGIS Pro Foundations

## Overview

Day 05 focused on geodata formats, geospatial data infrastructure, and geospatial services within the ArcGIS Pro environment.

The day concluded the first training week by extending the understanding of geospatial data from individual datasets toward the broader infrastructure and services used to provide and manage geographic information.

## Topics Covered

### 1. Geodata Formats

- Introduction to common geospatial data formats
- Understanding how geographic information is stored
- Working with different forms of geospatial datasets
- Relationship between data formats and GIS workflows

### 2. Geospatial Data Infrastructure

- Introduction to geospatial data infrastructure
- Organization and accessibility of geospatial information
- Understanding how datasets are provided within a GIS environment
- Relationship between local project data and broader geospatial infrastructure

### 3. Geospatial Services

- Introduction to geospatial services
- Understanding how geographic information can be provided through services
- Accessing geospatial information within GIS workflows
- Role of services in modern GIS environments
