
# Week 02 — Coordinate Systems, Map Scale & Data Capture

**Training period:** 13.07.2026 – 17.07.2026

Week 02 expanded the ArcGIS Pro foundations established during Week 01.

The training focused on coordinate systems and reference systems, map projections, scale and grids, data capture and digitization, data management, geodatabase topology, and geodatabase-based workflows.

## Week 02 Outcome

Week 02 established a stronger understanding of how spatial reference, cartographic representation, data creation, data management, and quality control interact within professional GIS workflows.

The week also introduced workflows that connect ArcGIS Pro with external geospatial data sources and structured geodatabase environments.
