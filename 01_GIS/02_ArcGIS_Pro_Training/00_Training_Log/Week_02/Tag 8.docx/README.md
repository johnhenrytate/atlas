## Overview

Day 08 focused on data capture and digitization workflows in ArcGIS Pro.

The training continued previous exercises involving map scale and grids and introduced practical editing and digitization workflows.

Features were edited and created for the preparation of enumeration areas.

The referenced Esri exercise focuses on creating and modifying features that can support census workers in Zambia.
