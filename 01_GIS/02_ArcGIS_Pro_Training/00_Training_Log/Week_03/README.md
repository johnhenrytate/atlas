
# Week 03 — Geoprocessing, Joins, Spatial Analysis & Georeferencing

**Training period:** 20.07.2026 – 24.07.2026

Week 03 focused on advanced geoprocessing and spatial data workflows in ArcGIS Pro.

The training covered batch geoprocessing, annotation classes, connections and relationships, SQL queries, joins and relates, spatial joins, commonly used geoprocessing tools, overlay and proximity analysis, data transfer, georeferencing, and geocoding.
