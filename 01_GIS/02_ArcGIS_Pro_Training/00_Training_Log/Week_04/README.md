
# Week 04 — Cartography, Labels, Geocoding & Visualization

**Training period:** 27.07.2026 – 31.07.2026

Week 04 focused on the visualization and cartographic representation of geospatial data in ArcGIS Pro.

The training covered thematic map symbolization, static and interactive maps, labeling, geocoding, GIS symbology, color design, thematic representations, diagrams, statistical outputs, and temporal visualization.
