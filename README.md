# ATLAS
## Geospatial Intelligence Platform

> An open geospatial intelligence platform for critical infrastructure analysis using GIS, spatial analytics, remote sensing, automation and open geospatial data.

---

## Vision

ATLAS is a long-term geospatial intelligence platform dedicated to the analysis of critical infrastructure, spatial resilience and decision support.

The platform integrates GIS, remote sensing, spatial analytics, automation, data engineering and open geospatial data to deliver reproducible, transparent and scalable geospatial intelligence.

---

## Mission

Develop a professional geospatial intelligence platform that documents the complete workflow from data acquisition to analysis, visualization and reporting.
